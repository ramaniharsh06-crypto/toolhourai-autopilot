# Platform safety — don't get flagged, don't lose income
What gets faceless accounts demonetized or banned, and what we do instead:
| Risk | Rule |
|---|---|
| "Reused/unoriginal content" (YouTube, TikTok, Meta) | Only original scripts, our own screen recordings, original voiceover. No compilations, no reuploads, no reaction-without-commentary. |
| Mass-produced / repetitive content | Rotate pillars; no template where only the text changes; max 2 posts/day/account. |
| Spam & inauthentic behaviour | No bots, no bought followers/likes/views, no follow-unfollow, no engagement pods, no auto-DMing strangers. Reply only to people who messaged/commented first. |
| Copyright strikes | Licensed or in-app music only; no movie/TV/other creators' clips; logos only in fair product demos. |
| Undisclosed AI / ads | AI label on; #ad on affiliate posts. |
| Misleading claims | No income promises; see compliance/claims.md. |
| Account linked bans | Each account created by the owner with real details + 2FA on. One account per platform. |
If ANY platform shows a warning, stop posting on that platform and escalate.
